# Edit Style Guide (from 5 reference reels)

Derived from the five sample videos (all 720×1280, 9:16, 30 fps, 43–133 s).
Format: Tamil talking-head creator reels, shot at a desk against a wood cabinet backdrop.

## Structure
- **Hook in the first 1–2 s.** Open mid-sentence and drop a big number or question graphic straight away (an animated "3" badge top-left, "?" marks popping around the head).
- **Silence removed with tight jump cuts.** No pauses longer than ~0.2 s are left between phrases.
- **Mid-roll call to action:** "Don't forget to follow…" around 15–40 % of the way in, shown with a follow-button card.
- **Close:** a short follow or "see you in the next reel" line, with no outro card.

## Keyframes / camera
- **Alternating punch-ins at cuts:** wide (1.0×) ↔ punched in (~1.15–1.25×), so every jump cut feels like a new angle.
- **Slow push-in** on emphasis lines (a gentle zoom over 2–3 s).
- **Whip / motion-blur transition** into new sections.
- **Desaturate to B&W** for 1–2 s on a negative or "warning" line, then snap back to color.

## Motion graphics (subtle, small, fast)
- **Number badge** ("3") top-left, with a hand-drawn circle or scribble reveal.
- **Animated stat counters:** pink "FOLLOWS 690 → 4200" pills that count up and pop in pairs.
- **Follow-button card:** a white pill with an avatar, `@handle` and a Follow button that flips to "Following".
- **Keyword text callouts** at chest height, showing one or two keywords per beat ("hook first", "quality / quantity").
- **Profile screenshot / phone mockup** inserts as proof points.
- **B-roll cutaways** (stock footage or movie clips) with keyword titles in a serif + sans pairing ("completely **selfmade**").

## Captions (the translated layer)
- The speaker talks in **Tamil**; the captions are **English translations**.
- **Two-line layout** at the lower-middle of the frame (~75–80 % down, above the UI safe zone):
  - Line 1: **white**, bold sans, with a soft shadow.
  - Line 2: **yellow (#F5E50A-ish)**, same weight, slightly smaller.
- **Phrase-level timing** (one spoken clause per card), not word-by-word.
- An alternate style used in one reel: single transliterated words in a white condensed font (Tanglish), at the center.

## Audio
- All references are mastered to **−14 LUFS integrated** (the social-platform standard).
- Clean voice; light background music under the B-roll; whoosh SFX on the whip transitions.

## Plan for the raw video
1. Transcribe → cut silences and flubs (keep natural breaths, ~0.1–0.15 s padding).
2. Translate the transcript into English captions in the two-line white/yellow style.
3. Alternate the punch-in zoom on each cut, add a push-in on key lines, and occasional B&W emphasis.
4. Add graphics where the script supports them: number badge, counters for stats, follow card at the CTA, keyword callouts.
5. Audio: high-pass, gentle denoise, compression, loudness-normalize to −14 LUFS.
6. B-roll only from footage you supply. Copyrighted movie clips are not sourced.
