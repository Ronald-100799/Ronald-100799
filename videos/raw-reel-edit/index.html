If you're stuck in a skinny fat body type,don't skip this video.
There are two main reasonsfor being skinny fat.
Number one:lack of resistance training.
Doing more cardio or sportswon't improve your body composition.
Instead, you need to focuson building muscle.
Train to increase your strength,and your muscle building improves,
and your body compositionimproves along with it.
Focus on building muscle and yourbody composition improves,
and you'll gainmore confidence.
My suggestion: follow a3–4 day workout split per week.
It gives you plentyof time to recover.
Also, don't forgetprogressive overload every week.
Add compound liftsto your workout.
Bench press, squat, deadlift —lifts like these.
They let you activate and targetmore muscle groups at once.
Reason number two:poor diet.
Too much processed andsugary food in your diet
means you can't build muscle, and yourbody composition won't improve.
Instead of processed andsugary foods,
focus onprotein-rich whole foods.
Protein-rich whole foods helpyour muscle building
and yourhormonal balance.
Good fat sources like nutsalso help your hormonal balance.
If you're skinny fat, knowing yourmaintenance calories is key.
If you're above 15% body fat,eat 100–200 calories
below yourmaintenance calories.
If you're below 15% body fat, eat atmaintenance or a small surplus —
about 100 caloriesabove your maintenance.
That helps you build musclewithout gaining much fat.
One last important thing:if you're skinny fat,
patience is everything.Stick with this for the long run.
If you're impatient, you'll getdemotivated and give up.
So lower your expectations,focus on progressive overload
and your diet, and you'lldefinitely get results.
