Maintenance calories are what keep
your current body weight the same —
the amount you need to eat every day.
Consistently eat more and your weight goes up.
Consistently eat less and it comes down.
The key word:
CONSISTENTLY
But how do you estimate your maintenance calories?
Ask ChatGPT for a rough estimate.
It takes your details and gives you a number.
With a food scale and a calorie-tracking app,
eat that many calories daily — and track it.
Snacks, breakfast, oils — everything.
Weigh yourself every morning on an empty stomach,
and take the weekly average.
If your weight drops on ChatGPT's number,
your maintenance is a little higher than that.
