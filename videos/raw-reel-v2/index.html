2 MAIN REASONS
Number one: lack of resistance training.
Doing more

MUSCLE BUILDING
When you focus on muscle building,

My suggestion: follow a
3–4 day workout split each week.

And every week, don't forget progressive overload.
Add compound lifts to your workout —
bench press, squat, deadlift.
Lifts like these
let you activate and target
MORE MUSCLEGROUPS
at the same time.
Reason number two: poor diet.
If your diet has too much processed and sugary food,
you can't
BUILD MUSCLE
and your body composition won't improve.
Instead of processed and sugary foods,
focus on
PROTEIN-RICHWHOLE FOODS
Eating protein-rich whole foods helps
your muscle building
and your hormonal balance.
Good fat sources like nuts
GOOD FATS
also support your hormonal balance.
And if you're skinny fat,
knowing your
MAINTENANCECALORIES
is really important.
If you're above 15% body fat,
eat 100–200 calories less
than your maintenance calories.
If you're below 15% body fat,
eat at maintenance, or a small surplus —
about 100 calories above maintenance.
That supports your muscle building
and helps you avoid extra fat gain.
One last important thing:
if you're skinny fat,
you need
PATIENCE
Keep doing all of this for an extended period of time.
If you're impatient,
you'll get demotivated and give up.
So lower your expectations,
focus on progressive overload
and on your diet —
and you'll definitely get results.
